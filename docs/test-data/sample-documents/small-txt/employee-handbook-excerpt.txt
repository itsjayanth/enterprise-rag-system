Employee Handbook Excerpt
=========================

Paid Time Off
-------------
Employees must submit planned PTO requests at least 10 business days in advance.
Managers should approve or reject the request within 3 business days.

Remote Work
-----------
Hybrid employees may work remotely on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday with manager approval.
All team members are expected to be in the office on Fridays for planning and team coordination.

Expenses
--------
Any single expense above $500 requires finance approval before purchase.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.

Physical Security
-----------------
Lost employee badges must be reported to workplace security within 1 hour.
