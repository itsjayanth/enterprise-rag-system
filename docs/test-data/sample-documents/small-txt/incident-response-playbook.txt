Incident Response Playbook
==========================

Severity Levels
---------------
A Severity 1 incident affects a critical customer-facing workflow or causes a full outage.
A Severity 1 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.

Coordination
------------
The on-call lead assigns the incident commander.
During active incidents, the primary Slack coordination channel is #incident-war-room.
Status updates must be posted every 30 minutes until mitigation is complete.

Post-Incident Work
------------------
A postmortem draft must be completed within 5 business days.
Action items should include an owner, due date, and rollback or prevention plan.
